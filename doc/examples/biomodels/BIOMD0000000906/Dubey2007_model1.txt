theeffect of environmental toxicant on the immune response of the body. Criteria 
for localstability, instability and global stability are obtained. It is shown that 
the immuneresponse of the body decreases as the concentration of environmental toxicant 
increases,and certain criteria are obtained under which it settles down at its equilibrium 
level.In the absence of toxicant, an oscillatory behavior of immune system and pathogenicgrowth 
is observed. However, in the presence of toxicant, oscillatory behavior is notobserved.
