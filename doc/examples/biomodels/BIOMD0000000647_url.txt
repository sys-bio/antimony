Abstract:


This paper investigates the influence of the Raf Kinase Inhibitor Pro- tein (RKIP)
on the Extracellular signal Regulated Kinase (ERK) signaling pathway through mathematical
modeling and simulation. Using nonlinear ordi- nary differential equations
to represent biochemical reactions in the pathway, we suggest a technique for
parameter estimation, utilizing time series data of proteins involved in the 
signaling pathway. The mathematical model allows the simulation the sensitivity
of the ERK pathway to variations of initial RKIP and ERK-PP (phosphorylated ERK)
concentrations along with time. Throughout the simulation study, we can qualitatively
validate the proposed mathematical model compared with experimental results.
