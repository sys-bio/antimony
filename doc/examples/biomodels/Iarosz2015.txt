R. LopesAbstract: In recent years, it became clear that a better understanding of
the interactions among the main elements involved in the cancer network is necessary
for the treatment of cancer and the suppression of cancer growth. In this work we
propose a system of coupled differential equations that model brain tumour under
treatment by chemotherapy, which considers interactions among the glial cells, the
glioma, the neurons, and the chemotherapeutic agents. We study the conditions for
the glioma growth to be eliminated, and identify values of the parameters for which
