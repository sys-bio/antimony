**A quantitative comparison of Calvin–Benson cycle models** 
Anne Arnold, Zoran Nikoloski *Trends in Plant Science* 2011
Oct 14. [22001849](http://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/22001849) , 
**Abstract:** 
The Calvin-Benson cycle (CBC) provides the precursors for biomass synthesis
necessary for plant growth. The dynamic behavior and yield of the CBC depend on the
environmental conditions and regulation of the cellular state. Accurate quantitative
models hold the promise of identifying the key determinants of the tightly regulated
CBC function and their effects on the responses in future climates. We provide an
integrative analysis of the largest compendium of existing models for photosynthetic
processes. Based on the proposed ranking, our framework facilitates the discovery
of best-performing models with regard to metabolomics data and of candidates for
metabolic engineering.
