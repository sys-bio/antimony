**Abstract:** 
Secretion of insulin by electrically coupled populations of pancreatic beta-cells
is governed by bursting electrical activity. Isolated beta -cells,however, exhibit
atypical bursting or continuous spike activity. We studybursting as an emergent property
of the population, focussing on interactionsamong the subclass of spiking cells.
These are modelled by equipping the fastsubsystem with a saddle-node-loop bifurcation,
which makes it monostable. Suchcells can only spike tonically or remain silent when
isolated, but can beinduced to burst with weak diffusive coupling. With stronger
coupling, the cellsrevert to tonic spiking. We demonstrate that the addition of noise
dramaticallyincreases, via a phenomenon like stochastic resonance, the coupling range
overwhich bursting is seen. Copyright 2000 Academic Press.
