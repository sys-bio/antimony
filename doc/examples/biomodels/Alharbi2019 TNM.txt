been simulated to be 87% per day from a natural food source. Even though the response 
of the immune system is weak, the daily consumption of enough vitamins can play an 
essential role in delaying the development of an early stage of cancer. This study 
contributes to the increasing awareness regarding a healthy diet to reduce the risk
