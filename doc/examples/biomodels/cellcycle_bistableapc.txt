Models the production and degradation of cyclin B that drives the early embryonic
cell cycle.Cyclin B is degraded by APC/C. The activity of APC/C is modelled not through
biochemical interactions, but through a "functional response curve". This
can be ultrasensitive (with the parameter alpha = 0). In this case, the system does
not oscillate. Importantly, the response can be made bistable and the form of the
bistability can be easily prescribed. With a bistable response, the system oscillates.The
uploaded file corresponds to the model used for Figs.3H, I in the publication.
