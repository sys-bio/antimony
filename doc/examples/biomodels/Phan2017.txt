The complexity of the immune responses is a major challenge in current virotherapy.
This study incorporates the innate immune response into our basic model for virotherapy
and investigates how the innate immunity affects the outcome of virotherapy. The
viral therapeutic dynamics is largely determined by the viral burst size, relative
innate immune killing rate, and relative innate immunity decay rate. The innate immunity
