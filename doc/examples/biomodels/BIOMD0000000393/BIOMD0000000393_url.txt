**Abstract:** 
The Calvin-Benson cycle (CBC) provides the precursors for biomass synthesis necessary
for plant growth. The dynamic behavior and yield of the CBC depend on the environmental
conditions and regulation of the cellular state. Accurate quantitative models hold
the promise of identifying the key determinants of the tightly regulated CBC function
and their effects on the responses in future climates. We provide an integrative
analysis of the largest compendium of existing models for photosynthetic processes.
Based on the proposed ranking, our framework facilitates the discovery of best-performing
models with regard to metabolomics data and of candidates for metabolic engineering.
