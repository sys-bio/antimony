slope factors in the separate and common pathways. The following specific predictions
are made: (1) when the common pathway is steep, curves undergo potentiation and flattening;

when the common pathway is hyperbolic, curves undergo right-shift, with no slope
change; (4) when the slope depends on the separate pathways, curves only undergo
