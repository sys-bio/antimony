  // Notes:
  model notes ```
Fluid-phase endoeytosis (pinocytosis) kinetics were studied in Dictyostelium discoideum
amoebae from the axenic strain Ax-2 that exhibits high rates of fluid-phase endoeytosis
when cultured in liquid nutrient media. Fluorescein-labelled dextran (FITC-dextran)
was used as a marker in continuous uptake- and in pulse-chase exocytosis experiments.
In the latter case, efflux of the marker was monitored on cells loaded for short
periods of time and resuspended in marker-free medium. A multicompartmental model
was developed which describes satisfactorily fluid-phase endocytosis kinetics. In
particular, it accounts correctly for the extended latency period before exocytosis
in pulse-chase experiments and it suggests the existence of some sorts of maturation
stages in the pathway.
