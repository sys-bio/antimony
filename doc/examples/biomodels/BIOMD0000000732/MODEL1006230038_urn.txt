**Abstract:** 
A number of lines of evidence suggest that immunotherapy with the cytokineinterleukin-2
(IL-2) may boost the immune system to fight tumors. CD4+ T cells,the cells that orchestrate
the immune response, use these cytokines as signalingmechanisms for immune-response
stimulation as well as lymphocyte stimulation,growth, and differentiation. Because
tumor cells begin as &apos;self&apos;, the immunesystem may not respond in an effective
way to eradicate them. Adoptive cellularimmunotherapy can potentially restore or
enhance these effects. We illustratethrough mathematical modeling the dynamics between
tumor cells, immune-effectorcells, and IL-2. These efforts are able to explain both
short tumor oscillationsin tumor sizes as well as long-term tumor relapse. We then
explore the effectsof adoptive cellular immunotherapy on the model and describe under
whatcircumstances the tumor can be eliminated.
