**Abstract:** 
In this paper we consider a model for the phenomenon of bursting in nerve cells.
Experimental evidence indicates that this phenomenon is due to the interaction of
multiple conductances with very different kinetics, and the model incorporates this
evidence. As a parameter is varied the model undergoes a transition between two oscillatory
waveforms; a corresponding transition is observed experimentally. After establishing
the periodicity of the subcritical oscillatory solution, the nature of the transition
is studied. It is found to be a resonance bifurcation, with the solution branching
at the critical point to another periodic solution of the same period. Using this
result a comparison is made between the model and experimental observations. The
model is found to predict and allow an interpretation of these observations.
