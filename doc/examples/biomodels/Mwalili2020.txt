spreading from person-to-person caused by a novel coronavirus and poses a serious
public health risk. The goal of this study was to apply a modified susceptible-exposed-infectious-recovered
(SEIR) compartmental mathematical model for prediction of COVID-19 epidemic dynamics
incorporating pathogen in the environment and interventions. The next generation
matrix approach was used to determine the basic reproduction number R0. The model
equations are solved numerically using fourth and ffth order Runge–Kutta methods.
Results: We found an R0 of 2.03, implying that the pandemic will persist in the human
population in the absence of strong control measures. Results after simulating various
